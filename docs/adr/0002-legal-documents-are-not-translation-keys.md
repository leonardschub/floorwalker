# Legal documents are not translation keys

> Amended by the [local-only spec](../../.scratch/local-only/spec.md) (2026-10-20): the app publishes one legal document now, the open-source licences page. The terms of service, privacy policy, EULA and imprint are dropped (there is no service, and AGPL-3.0-or-later replaces the EULA). The decision stands for the licences page; the licence text is the repository's `LICENSE`, shown as it is, and the About section's credit and notices are interface strings.

The five legal documents ship as complete documents rendered at their own routes and stay outside `packages/i18n`, where every other user-facing string lives. Their prose, headings and version history have to be read as one artifact by an operator and whoever reviews it; splitting that into flat string keys would break the document apart and tie its structure to the interface language. The trade-off accepted here is that document language and interface language move independently — a document declares the language it is written in, and translating one means adding a whole document rather than adding keys. This is worth recording because the rest of the codebase establishes the opposite habit, and a future reader would otherwise treat the missing keys as an oversight.
